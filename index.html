A small group of founders from different industries meets in a private room to work through real problems and find ways to build together.

What happens at the table

Four things founders come away with.

Collective Intelligence

Business Breakthroughs

Bring a decision you are stuck on. Leave with a clearer next step.

Partnerships between businesses that would not usually meet.

Who sits at the table

Any industry. Funding is not a requirement.

House rules

Five rules for everyone at the table.

  1. I.

    Confidential conversations.

    What is said at the table stays there.

  2. II.

    No unsolicited sales pitches.

    Do not pitch unless someone asks.

  3. III.

    No passive spectators.

    Everyone in the room takes part.

  4. IV.

    Everyone contributes.

    An insight, a question or an introduction.

  5. V.

    Give as much as you take.

    Help as much as you hope to be helped.
